01 / ABOUT

More than finding bugs.
I engineer confidence.

I am a Senior QA Engineer with extensive experience across manual testing, automation, API testing, integration testing and release quality. I enjoy working where product thinking, engineering and testing meet.

My approach goes beyond checking whether a feature works. I think about risk, user journeys, failure modes, data, integrations, observability and how the team can prevent defects earlier.

I also enjoy mentoring testers and sharing practical QA knowledge through teaching, content and one-to-one guidance.
